Ichimoku data & settings: 

inside Cloud = no trade zone
price out of cloud: entry signal (break of sup/resistance)

above cloud: longs
below cloud: shorts

cloud = support/resistance

flat lines = 50% fib

TK cross:
bullish blue above red
bearish red above blue

lagging span:
want: lagging span above cloud in bullish trend, lagging span above price in bullish trend
lagging span in price - consolidation

kumo twist:
areas are easy to break/fall cause low support/resistance
they indicate early price reversal
if everything settles up for long trade but cloud still bearish.. the twist into bull cloud indicates the entry point

complement:
fib retrace/extension, RSI

Entry checklist long:
price above cloud yes/no
bullish cloud yes/no
bullish tk cross yes/no
lagging span above cloud, above price yes/no

Entry checklist short:
price below cloud yes/no
bearish cloud yes/no
bearish tk cross yes/no
lagging span below price yes/no
